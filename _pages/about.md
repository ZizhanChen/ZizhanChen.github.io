I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at **The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)**, working with Prof. Zili Shao. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from CUHK in 2024, and my B.S. in Computer Science from Xi'an Jiaotong University in 2019 (Outstanding Graduate, Rank 4/160).

My research lies at the intersection of **mobile and embedded systems**, **operating systems (Android)**, and **system performance optimization**. I am particularly interested in:

- **Display Management & Energy Efficiency** – Semantics-aware multi-screen adaptation, Q-learning-based display energy optimization, and transparent layout rearrangement for foldable and dual-screen devices.
- **Runtime Optimization & System Reliability** – Transparent handling of runtime configuration changes (RCHDroid), display-centric process models to mitigate UI latency, and system-level solutions for lifecycle management bugs.
- **Cross-Device & Intelligent Interaction** – Multi-screen development frameworks (MSA), generalized LLM-mobile interfaces, and privacy-preserving frameworks for WebView security.

During my Ph.D. and postdoctoral tenure, I have designed and implemented system-level solutions that have been published in top-tier venues including **ASPLOS, TCAD, TACO, LCTES, and TKDE**. My work has contributed to improving the responsiveness, energy efficiency, and development agility of Android-based mobile systems.

I am also actively exploring the integration of large language models with mobile applications, aiming to enable more capable and secure mobile AI agents. I am passionate about building practical, impactful systems that bridge the gap between hardware capabilities and user experience.
