This journal paper extends our ASPLOS work on RCHDroid, providing a comprehensive evaluation and further optimizations for transparent runtime configuration handling in Android. As the sole first author, Zizhan Chen proposed the research motivation, validated the scientific problem, designed the solution and experiments, and completed the manuscript. Published in TACO 2025.
