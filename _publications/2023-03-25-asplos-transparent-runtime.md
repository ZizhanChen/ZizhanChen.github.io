This paper presents RCHDroid, a transparent system-level approach that handles runtime configuration changes for Android apps without requiring developer intervention, thereby eliminating common lifecycle management bugs. As the sole first author, Zizhan Chen proposed the research motivation, validated the scientific problem, designed the solution and experiments, and completed the manuscript. Published at ASPLOS 2023.
