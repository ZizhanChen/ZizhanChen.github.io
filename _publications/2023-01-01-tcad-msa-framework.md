This work presents MSA, a framework that enables Android apps to support multiple screens transparently, easing development and improving cross-device compatibility. As the sole first author, Zizhan Chen proposed the research motivation, validated the scientific problem, designed the solution and experiments, and completed the manuscript. Published in TCAD 2023.
