Groundhog is a fine-grained in-storage processing framework designed to accelerate spatio-temporal data analytics. As the sole corresponding author, Zizhan Chen identified the existing problems, proposed and validated the hypotheses, formulated the solution, designed the experiments, drafted the abstract and introduction, and revised and polished the entire manuscript. Published in TKDE 2026.
