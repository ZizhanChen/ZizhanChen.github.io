This paper introduces a reinforcement learning approach to dynamically adjust display energy consumption in Android devices, achieving energy savings without harming user experience. As the sole first author, Zizhan Chen proposed the research motivation, validated the scientific problem, designed the solution and experiments, and completed the manuscript. Published in TCAD 2024.
