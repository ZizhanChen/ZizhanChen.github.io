This work proposes a semantics-aware display management scheme for dual-screen foldable Android devices, achieving significant energy savings without degrading user experience. As the sole first author, Zizhan Chen proposed the research motivation, validated the scientific problem, designed the solution and experiments, and completed the manuscript. Published at LCTES 2022.
